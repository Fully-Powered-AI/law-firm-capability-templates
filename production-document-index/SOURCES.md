Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. From the discovery document summary method in CaseMark/skills (Apache-2.0) it takes the idea of giving each produced document its own entry with its production number, date, author, recipients and type, a brief account of its contents and exact quotations located by Bates page; opening with a count of what the production holds and the span of its dates; putting a Bates page behind each statement; keeping material that may be privileged out of the summary and leaving it for the lawyer; noting confidentiality markings under a protective order; leaving doubtful or clashing passages as they stand; linking documents to one another; and listing documents that are mentioned but absent. Fully Powered AI wrote the version that starts only from counsel's written clearance and counsel's own screen list, holds a whole family by Bates number when any member matches that list, accounts for every Bates number in the cleared range, checks the export's columns before reading, keeps to five named kinds of link, records differences as two quoted passages without a verdict, drops every judgment of relevance or significance, heads the draft for a named lawyer with a line for each point to confirm, and declines to assess, advise or draft. No third-party text is reused.
