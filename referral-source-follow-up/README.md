# Referral source follow-up

The day after a partner meets someone who sends the firm work, they have a follow-up draft and a list of every promise made at the table, by both sides. The draft uses only approved materials and nothing said in confidence; the partner checks it, chooses the next step and sends it.

*Workflow template · Law firms · Sales · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflows/referral-source-follow-up/)*

## The outcome

Every promise made over lunch gets kept, or at least gets a decision. A partner comes back from a conversation with an accountant, a banker, another lawyer or a prospective client, and by the next morning has a short message ready in their own voice: the checklist they offered, attached; the seminar date someone on the team owes, restated; the next step they want, proposed. That is how a fully powered practice turns a good lunch into the next introduction, instead of leaving it to a partner's memory.

What could embarrass the firm stays out of the message and is put in front of the partner instead: a client's deal mentioned in passing, news shared off the record, a banker's name before the banker has agreed to an introduction, a figure the firm's advertising rules control, a question that would need advice. The partner checks confidentiality and the advertising rules, edits, and sends. The workflow template never sends, never records and never answers a question on the law.

## Who starts the work, and when

The partner starts it within a day or two of the conversation, or hands their notes to a business development or relationship coordinator who runs it for them. It runs once per conversation, whether the conversation was a lunch, a call, a meeting or a word at an event.

## What they supply

- **Their notes from the conversation,** with the date and who was there, and any line marked confidential or not public.
- **The contact's row from the firm's contact list,** including how they like to be reached and whether they may be contacted at all.
- **The earlier history with this contact,** so older promises still open are not forgotten.
- **The firm's approved materials list,** with the audience each item is approved for and the date its approval ends.
- **A few settings:** the follow-up window, the word limit, the firm's own advertising checklist if it has one, and the next step the partner wants, if they have chosen it.

## What comes out

One page for the partner. At the top, when the follow-up is due and whether the draft is ready. Then the promises: the firm's, each marked as kept in the draft, restated, or left out with the reason; the contact's, each with a date to check back; anything said with a "maybe", kept apart from real commitments; and earlier promises still open. Then up to three next-step options taken from the notes, what was held back from the draft and why, any sentence that touches the advertising checklist, and any question the partner must answer personally. Then the draft itself, with its word count, and one proposed line for the relationship record.

A good run leaves the partner editing for tone, not hunting through their notes for what they said they would do.

## What has to change for your company

Replace the default advertising checklist with the categories your own rules and policies require, and add any wording they require on a message like this. Decide how many working days a follow-up may wait and how long a message may run. Agree who approves materials and keep the approved list current, with an end date on every item; the workflow template will offer nothing that is not on it. Decide what else your firm holds back, such as anything about a pitch or a panel. Match the column names to your own contact list and relationship history, or the workflow template will stop and ask. It runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini from the files and notes a person supplies, with no connection to the firm's mailbox or systems.

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
3. Give your AI assistant the instruction block and the output format from the template, with your own limits in place of the ones the instructions leave open in square brackets. Paste them in as custom instructions in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, or copy this whole folder into a product that loads `SKILL.md` packages. This folder stands alone; you do not need the rest of the repository.
