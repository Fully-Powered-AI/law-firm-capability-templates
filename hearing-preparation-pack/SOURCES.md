From the hearing preparation method in CaseMark/skills (Apache-2.0) it takes the shape of a pack that opens with the particulars of the hearing; sets out how the case reached it and the earlier rulings that touch it; gives each expected witness's earlier sworn statements with page references; inventories the exhibits with whether each has been pre-marked and whether the parties have agreed on it; ends with a checklist of what must be done before the hearing; ties every fact to the document it came from; and calls out what is absent, such as documents or statements nobody has supplied. Fully Powered AI took out the method's analysis of issues, legal standards, examination questions, notes on witness credibility and strategic assessment, and wrote the version that copies the hearing details from the notice and the history from the docket, quotes each listed order's directives, reports exhibit and checklist status only as the firm's own tracker and checklist record it, brings no checklist of its own, proposes a counted date only under a counsel-approved rule file with every day shown, keeps an open-items list with owners and no recommendations, adds a deposition variant with no question outline, heads everything as a draft for the named attorney with a line for each point to confirm, and declines to assess, advise or draft.
