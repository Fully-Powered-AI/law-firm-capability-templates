# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. One published method shaped it: the chronology workflow in anthropics/claude-for-legal (Apache-2.0). From it come the privilege-screening question, put to the attorney and answered on the record before any document is read; the question about documents obtained from another party; a single entry for an event however many documents record it, citing all of them; disagreements between documents left for counsel rather than settled; a gaps list covering empty periods, missing material and sources that could not be read; and numbered rebuilds that list what changed since the earlier version. That method also ranks each event by its weight for one side’s case; this workflow template leaves out every such ranking and leaves relevance and significance to the attorney. Fully Powered AI added, from its own published map of legal work, the rule that a document’s own date and the date of the event it records are kept apart, and added approximate and relative dates left as written, entries that quote and locate rather than describe, the Bates sequence check, the list of documents referred to but not in the set, legends copied as printed, and the attorney’s verification list. No third-party text is reused.
