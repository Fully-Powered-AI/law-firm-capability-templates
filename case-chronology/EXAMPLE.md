# Case chronology from the matter documents: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Quarrender Vale LLP, its people, its client Hollowmere Bakeries Inc., the other party Vantry Ovenworks Ltd, everyone named in the documents, the Eastvale Commercial Court, the case number, the protective order and every document below are invented. Nothing here is a real dispute, and nothing in it says who is right.

The paralegal’s three inputs come first, in full, then what the workflow template prepares. The handling attorney’s instruction answers both opening questions, on privilege screening and on documents from the other side, so the AI assistant records her answers rather than asking. Her screening answer speaks of the HOL documents only, so the draft says, rather than guesses, that it does not cover the two VAN documents. Today’s date is Wednesday 11 November 2026. The firm has set no gap threshold or date convention, so the defaults apply: 30 calendar days, and no numeric date that could be read two ways is read.

## What to notice

- **Two dates, kept apart.** The 7 April visit (E5) is recorded in an email written two days later and in a report written the same day; each source keeps its own document date. The letter of 24 April (E9) appears only because the 3 June letter mentions it, so its document date is 3 June.
- **One merged visit, the differences kept in one place.** E5 quotes only the visit itself from D04 and D07; what each says happened during it is in C1, quoted in full there.
- **Nothing converted.** “The following week” in E2 and “within 10 working days of delivery” in D01 are left as the documents wrote them. “This morning” and “last Thursday” wait under DATES TO PLACE for Odile Ferrant, even though the email’s own date is known.
- **One event, two sources, one disagreement.** Both D04 and D07 state 7 April 2026, name Vantry and Hollowmere, and describe the commissioning visit, so they are merged. They differ on what happened during the visit, so C1 sets their words side by side and says nothing about which is right. D08 describes the commissioning too but gives it no date, so it is not merged or dated: its words join C1, marked as not dated in that document, and the attorney decides whether it is the same visit.
- **Several acts, one document, one date.** D01 records the order confirmed and names a person for each company, all on 3 February 2026, so E1 is one entry naming each act and quoting each passage. Its event line says the confirmation “names” Teodor Basque and Ilse Morrow; “Accepted for” stays inside the quote, and nothing says anyone “accepted” or “signed”, because those words state a legal effect the attorney weighs. D03 likewise names T. Basque, with “Received by” and “Delivered” kept inside the quote.
- **What the document records, not what happened.** A letter or email is written as who sent it to whom. Everything else is written as what its document records or reports: D03 “records” a crated oven, D04 and D07 “record” the visit, D05 “reports” the call and the fitted motor. The chronology never says in its own voice that an oven was delivered or a motor fitted. The letter of 24 April 2026 is described as D06 describes it, “our letter”, with no sender or recipient added, because the set does not hold it.
- **Names as the documents give them.** D05 says only “Ilse”, so E8, P1 and P2 say “Ilse” and do not supply a surname the email does not give.
- **Places as the documents number them.** None of these documents numbers its paragraphs, so each place is the Bates page alone; nothing is called a “second paragraph”.
- **No ranking.** No event is called key, and none is marked as helping or hurting Hollowmere or Vantry. That reading is Odile Ferrant’s.
- **Privilege as she stated it.** Her answer is recorded word for word. Every entry drawn from D04 is marked. Her answer speaks of the HOL documents only, so D07 and D08 are listed as “screening not stated in the attorney’s answer”, each with a line for her to answer, and are not called screened. The “CONFIDENTIAL” legend on D05 is copied as printed, and nobody but the attorney decides what it means.
- **The arithmetic.** The gap from Friday 24 April to Wednesday 3 June 2026 is 6 days left in April, 31 in May and 3 in June: 40 days. Every other interval between exact-dated events is 29 days or less (2 March to 31 March), so it is the only gap over 30 days. The period begins on E1’s date and ends on E10’s, so there is no stretch at either end to list.
