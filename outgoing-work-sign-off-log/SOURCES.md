# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. The idea comes from the supervision queue in the law-school clinic plugin published in anthropics/claude-for-legal (Apache-2.0), and from its adaptation for clinics in England and Wales in uk-agents/uk-legal-plugins (Apache-2.0). In both, a student's work is held until the supervising lawyer approves it or sends it back with a comment, the most pressing items are shown first, and each decision is logged; the uk-agents version records who made each decision and when, and treats that record as evidence that supervision took place. Fully Powered AI turned the idea into a status log for a firm's operations manager: it never opens or judges the work, orders items by the date they must go out with a warning window counted in working days, accepts a decision only from the named attorney's own message, calls out work sent before approval and decisions passed on second-hand, works from an exported log with stated column headers, and proposes the updated log and its history lines for a person to check and save. No third-party text is reused.

- **Fully Powered AI:** The firm version of the work: status only, with no draft read or judged; items ordered by send-by date with a warning window in working days; decisions accepted only from the named attorney's own message or a named covering attorney; flags for work sent before approval, decisions passed on second-hand and items with no attorney or no date; an exported log with stated column headers and a stop on any mismatch; and a proposed update with history lines that a person checks and saves.
