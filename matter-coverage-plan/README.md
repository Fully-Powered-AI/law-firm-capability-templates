# Matter coverage plan for absences

When leave or a staffing change takes someone away from active matters, the practice manager gets a draft coverage plan: a proposed cover for each matter, the attorney-confirmed dates in the absence, the access each cover needs and every handoff that is blocked. The practice lead and responsible attorneys decide.

*Workflow template · Law firms · Operations and process improvement · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflows/matter-coverage-plan/)*

## The outcome

The aim is that no matter goes dark because its lawyer is on leave. Before the absence starts, the practice lead holds one page that answers the questions that usually surface too late: who picks up each matter, what is already booked while the person is away, whether the cover can even open the file, and which handoffs the firm's own records block, and why.

Every proposed cover has passed the same four checks, in the same order, every time: not away themselves; named with that matter by no row of the conflicts team's screen list (a row the plan cannot read counts against everyone it might name); holding every qualification the practice lead requires for it; and inside the cover limit the practice lead set. Where nobody passes, the plan says so plainly and shows why, so the practice lead decides with the facts in front of them instead of discovering a barrier the week of a hearing.

This is preparation, and it stays preparation. It gives no legal advice. It decides nothing about legal meaning, merit, validity, privilege, conflicts or deadlines, and clears no one. It sends, files, signs and calendars nothing. It reads the screen list as supplied, and it never says that a plan, a cover or a check is complete or safe to rely on; asked for a verdict, it names the lawyer who decides and points to the line of the plan set out for them. Every date in the plan is copied from dates an attorney has already confirmed; it counts, moves and suggests none. It changes no assignment or access, and contacts no client. It writes no email, letter or notice for anyone, not even as a draft for a lawyer to edit. A case or rule cited inside one of the files is listed for a lawyer to verify and never applied. The plan is headed as a draft for the practice lead by name, with a line for each point a lawyer must confirm, including the conflict check for every person proposed to cover a matter.

## Who starts the work, and when

The practice manager starts it once leave is approved, or once the practice lead confirms a staffing change, and ideally a week or more before the absence begins. It can be run again whenever the leave dates, the team or the screen list change.

The practice lead, a lawyer, agrees or changes each proposed cover and decides every blocked handoff. If no practice lead is named, or the one named is the person away or is not recorded as a lawyer, nothing is prepared until the firm names the lawyer who decides. The responsible attorney on each matter confirms that the commitments list is complete and its dates right, the conflict check for anyone joining the matter, what the cover may do on the matter, and the access the cover needs. Where the responsible attorney is the person who is away, the practice lead, or a lawyer the firm names, confirms in their place. Every confirming lawyer must be recorded in the staff list as a lawyer, or that matter's confirm lines are addressed to no one until the firm names one. The plan flags any of these lawyers whose own leave falls in the absence, or whom the screen list names on a matter they decide or confirm. Matter-file access is decided by the person the firm names, and only after the confirming lawyer has confirmed the conflict check and scope and the practice lead has agreed the cover.

It is not for leave that is only requested, for telling a client who is covering their matter or writing to a court or anyone else about the absence, for deciding whether anyone has a conflict, or for the handover when someone leaves the firm.

## What they supply

- **The request:** who is away, today's date, the practice lead's name, the staff roles in the firm that only lawyers hold, and who confirms when the responsible attorney is the one away.
- **The leave record:** approved and requested leave for everyone in the team.
- **Three exports from the case system or practice records:** the matter list, who holds which role on each matter with its named backup, and the staff list with each person's qualifications and cover limit.
- **The role requirements file,** dated and approved by the practice lead: which staff roles may cover each matter role, and any qualification a particular matter needs in its cover.
- **The attorney-confirmed dates** for each matter.
- **The screen list** from the conflicts team, dated: who is recorded as screened from, or conflicted on, which matter.
- **The access record,** if the firm keeps one: who can open each matter file.

The workflow template states the column headers it expects in each export. If a header does not match, it stops and asks rather than guessing. It works from these files alone and needs no connection to the firm's systems and no sign-in details.

## What comes out

- **A short note to the practice manager:** what stopped, what is blocked, which dates to chase, which deciding lawyers are themselves away during the absence or named on the screen list for a matter they decide, and anything in the files that was not acted on, including any case cited in a file, listed for a lawyer to verify.
- **The draft plan,** marked as a draft for the practice lead by name, with the cover period and the window for commitments worked out and shown.
- **Matters affected,** in the order their first confirmed commitment falls, plus matters not planned and any matter where the absent person was the named backup, with its commitments in the window.
- **Proposed cover** for each role, with which step of the order found the person and each of the four checks.
- **Commitments in the absence,** copied word for word with who confirmed each and when; unconfirmed ones are marked, and ones without a date are listed as such.
- **Access needed,** for the person who decides access, after the lawyers' lines that come first.
- **Blocked handoffs,** with every person tested, every reason and the commitments left without cover, marked as above.
- **Capacity,** and anything in the files that could not be read.
- **A confirm list:** one line for the practice lead per cover and per blocked handoff, and a block for each confirming lawyer per matter, with a conflict check line for every proposed cover.
- **A check block** that adds the dates and the screen list back to their files row by row and says nothing was changed.

## What has to change for your company

The role requirements file is yours, and the plan is only as good as it. The practice lead decides which staff roles may cover which matter roles, whether cover must stay within the team, and which matters need a named qualification in their cover, written exactly as the staff list records it. Set each person's cover limit. Agree with the conflicts team how the screen list is produced and how old it may be. Name who confirms when the responsible attorney is the one away, who decides matter-file access, and where confirmations are recorded. Replace the column headers with the ones your exports really use.

We sit with the practice manager who arranges cover today and the practice lead who approves it, write down what is really done, and set it up in the AI product the firm has approved: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini. Your firm keeps control of how the work gets done.
