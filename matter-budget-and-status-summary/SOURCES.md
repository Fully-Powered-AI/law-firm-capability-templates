# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. Two openly licensed matter-reporting methods in CaseMark/skills (Apache-2.0) shaped it. From project-summary: a status report on one legal matter that rates progress red, amber or green and gives the cause of any rating that is not green, sets the original budget beside spend to date and a projected total, separates finished milestones from those still to come, and lists risks and actions each with an owner. From project-management-summary: a dated header saying who the report is for and how widely it may go, budget lines with baseline, spend, forecast to complete and variance, the decisions waiting on someone, and a closing account of the records used. Their team allocation percentages, quality and client-satisfaction ratings, privilege markings and legal analysis are not used. Fully Powered AI's own: rating only what the records measure, by fixed rules the firm sets, with every rule that fired printed with its figures; the tie-out against the time export; the stop on a plan that does not add up or a column header it cannot match; the partner's own rating shown beside the calculated one, never replacing it; risks and actions kept in the matter lead's words with no owner or date ever supplied; leaving out any view on the merits and saying so; the checks for the billing coordinator; and the rule that nothing is sent, billed or changed. No third-party text is reused.
