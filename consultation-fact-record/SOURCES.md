# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. The core of the method comes from the client-intake package in rohasnagpal/legal-ai-skills (MIT): reading the whole account once before structuring any of it, sorting what a client says by how the client came to know it, recording how precisely each date was given and leaving it that way, naming each person only as the client described them, keeping the result the client hopes for apart from the account, listing the papers the client spoke of but did not hand over without assuming what they contain, keeping both sides wherever the account disagrees with itself, and stopping before any legal analysis. Fully Powered AI added the rest: a mark for a statement whose basis the notes do not show; a pointer from every line back to its paragraph or timestamp in the notes; supplied documents listed by name and never read; names checked against the party list from the inquiry; time limits the client mentions quoted and never counted; the client's own questions kept for the attorney; the note-taker's comments left out and located; the neutral follow-up questions, which grow the source's list of assumptions for the lawyer to test into factual questions for every assumption, gap, contradiction, time limit and loosely dated event, each pointing back to its lines; the draft heading and the lines the intake attorney confirms. No third-party text is reused.
