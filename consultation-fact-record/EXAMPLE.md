# Consultation fact record: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The firm, its people, the prospective client, Shop A, Contractor B, Supplier C, Company D, every other person named and every figure below are invented. Nothing here describes a real consultation, a real business or a real dispute.

The paralegal's three inputs come first, in full, then what the workflow template prepares. The request carries the consultation details and says there is no firm format, and the second input holds both the party list and the list of supplied documents, so the five inputs the workflow template asks for arrive as three.

## Input 1: the request

Sent by Priya Lund, paralegal, on Wednesday 4 November 2026.

```
Please prepare the consultation fact record for Tomas Reyhill.

Prospective client: Ines Varga
Matter label: Varga / Contractor B, ventilation works
Consultation: video call, 4 November 2026, 10:00 to 10:50

Priya Lund, paralegal (notes)

square brackets.
No firm format: use the default.
```

Statement 3 gives no amount and no way of paying, because the notes give two of each and the record does not choose. Statement 11 sits after the letter of 30 October although "late September" comes earlier in the year. The client told it last of the dated statements, and it does not move ahead: "last week" before it could only be compared by working out a date, and "since late September" runs up to the consultation, so it overlaps every later date. D1 and D2 say "holder not stated" because the notes say only that the client will email them; the photo of the letter that she read from is noted on D1, not listed as a document of its own. D3 is held by the client because the notes say she has the photographs. Her plan to email the documents, her reading from the photo during the call and Tomas Reyhill's reply at ¶13 are not statements: the documents are in section 5, the words she read are T1, and her question is in section 4. The note-taker's comment at ¶14 is a view on the matter, so the record lists where it is and repeats none of it.

Tomas Reyhill checks each line against the notes, chooses the questions, answers the client's question himself, and decides whether the four new names go to the conflict review. Only after he signs does the record go into the intake file.
