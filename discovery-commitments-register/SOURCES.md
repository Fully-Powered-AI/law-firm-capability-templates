Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. From the discovery deficiency and conferral tracking method in CaseMark/skills (Apache-2.0) it takes the idea of a register with a line for every request on its own, holding the request and the response in their exact words with the date each was served, the Bates ranges produced and each later supplement, a dated record of every conferral with its form and the people present, and the undertakings each side gave there with their dates; a reference for each line that stays fixed as the record grows; and marking for the attorney anything the papers do not settle. The longer description of the same method in the same repository adds telling a firm undertaking apart from an agreement in principle, and noting where in a response set each passage sits. Fully Powered AI removed the method's classification of deficiencies, its deficiency statements and proposed cures, its relief wording, materiality and sanctions notes, its deadline counting and its client and court reports. It wrote the fixed rules that give every promise one standing against the as-of date and the production log, the overdue list, the note of whether a promise appears in the promising side's own document or only in a call note, the passages on scope shown with the side that wrote each, closures that only the attorney makes, the lines for the attorney to confirm and the check sums. No third-party text is reused.
