--- 124 ---
The buyer's duty is a short one to state. A buyer who finds a defect in

seller can still inspect the defect. The duty does not turn on the size of

Had someone supplied a made-up text for it, the worksheet would have compared that text like any other; that is why every authority stays on the good-law list.
