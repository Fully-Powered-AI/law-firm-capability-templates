# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. Two published methods shaped it. CaseMark/skills (Apache-2.0) supplied the idea of sorting each citation into verified and unverified categories rather than a pass or a fail, with room for a page that cannot be reached, and of keeping an unverified claim in view with a flag instead of deleting it; the rule that a quotation reproduces its source exactly, carrying the source's own mistakes marked [sic] and any shortening marked; the rule that a page reference is never made up; checking a record cite's Bates prefix and number of digits against the production's own stamps, and treating a page the production does not hold as a finding; a closing tally of the results; and a second hand check of a sample after the full pass. anthropics/claude-for-legal (Apache-2.0) supplied the idea of listing and counting every citation in a document before checking any of them, checking all of them rather than a few, reporting how many were checked out of how many were found, saying "could not check", never "confirmed", whenever an item's source text is missing, and testing whether a pinpoint backs every part of the sentence it is attached to. Fully Powered AI added the rest: four results named for what was compared with the supplied text and never called confirmed; every word at the place cited that the draft leaves out listed without choosing; every authority marked to be verified by the lawyer; a source list with fixed headings and a rule for whole and part sources; an authority's names, volume, page, court and year compared with its own title page or caption; quotations compared character by character with marked and unmarked changes kept apart; each part of a sentence quoted and located rather than interpreted; good-law checks left listed for the lawyer to run in the firm's own research service; a worksheet addressed to the named responsible lawyer that edits nothing in the draft and names no authority from memory; and the stops and refusals. No third-party text is reused.
