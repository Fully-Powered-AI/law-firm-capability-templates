# Citation and quotation check

Before a draft is filed or relied on, the associate or paralegal has one worksheet: every citation, quotation and record cite counted, then set against the sources supplied, and every good-law check left for the lawyer. No result calls anything confirmed; each says only what was compared.

*Workflow template · Law firms · Legal and contracts · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflows/citation-and-quotation-check/)*

## The outcome

The person doing the cite check stops hunting and starts deciding. Every item is found and counted first, so the worksheet opens with plain coverage, then gives each one of four results named for exactly what was compared: matches the supplied text, differs, partly matches the supplied text, or could not be checked. The draft’s words and the source’s words sit side by side, with every word at the place cited that the draft leaves out listed in full, so the signing lawyer sees each problem without reopening a file.

This is a safeguard against the invented citation. A citation no supplied source answers to is never quietly passed: it is marked to be verified by the lawyer and goes to the top, to be pulled in the firm’s research service before anyone relies on it. Quotations are compared character by character, so a dropped word or a tidied spelling shows up before the court does. What it cannot catch: if someone supplies a made-up text for an invented or wrong authority, the worksheet compares the draft with that text like any other. That is why every authority stays on the good-law list for the lawyer to check in the firm’s own research service.

The worksheet is preparation for a lawyer. It gives no legal advice. It decides nothing about legal meaning, merit, validity, privilege, conflicts or deadlines. It never says an authority is good law, current or binding: those checks are listed for the lawyer to run. It names no authority from memory, edits nothing in the draft, and sends, files, signs and calendars nothing. The responsible lawyer confirms accuracy, current validity and suitability before the work is filed or relied on.

## Who starts the work, and when

An associate or a litigation paralegal starts it when a draft is close to final and its sources have been pulled. The responsible lawyer who will sign is named on the request and confirms the worksheet line by line; in a company’s legal department that is in-house counsel. Where the draft comes from outside counsel and the company has no lawyer of its own, the worksheet is addressed to outside counsel and never handed to the business as an answer.

It is not for writing or rewriting any part of a draft, finding an authority, saying what an authority means, judging an argument, counting a deadline, or checking the house style of citations.

## What they supply

- **The draft,** or the section to check, with page and line numbers or numbered paragraphs, footnotes included.
- **A source list** with six columns, headed “Source ID”, “Cited in the draft as”, “What it is”, “File”, “Pages, lines or Bates range supplied” and “Whole source supplied”. If the headings differ, the work stops and asks.
- **The source texts:** authorities as printed from the firm’s research service, transcript pages, exhibits and the Bates-stamped production, whole or in part.
- **The request:** the matter, the draft and version, who is running the check, the responsible lawyer and the firm’s research service.

The workflow template works only from these documents. It brings in no authority, rule or summary from anywhere else.

## What comes out

One worksheet, headed as a draft for the named lawyer:

- **Coverage:** items found, items compared, and the count of each result.
- **Items:** the draft’s words beside the source’s words at the place cited, any quotation difference, each part of the sentence and whether the passage states it, every word and sentence at that place the draft leaves out, and the result.
- **Not in the sources supplied:** every citation with no matching source, marked to be verified by the lawyer and first in line to be pulled.
- **Good-law checks outstanding:** every authority cited, with a line for who ran the check and what it showed.
- **Lawyer to confirm:** a line for every open point, then completeness and suitability.

The person who ran it re-checks the problem items and a few matches by hand before the lawyer sees it. The draft’s owners make any corrections.

## What has to change for your company

Your firm decides who may run the check and who confirms it, names the research service where good-law checks are run, agrees the source list and whether a part source is enough for a pinpoint, and decides how many matches are re-checked by hand and where the signed worksheet is kept. Citation form, if checked, stays a separate step.
