# Inquiry handoff: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The inquiry as it arrived.

| Source | What it holds |
|----|----|
| Web form, Monday morning | Client A, a regional equipment distributor, has a contract dispute with Party B, a former supplier. Contact: Client A’s operations director. |
| Call, 12 minutes, same day | Party B ended the supply agreement and holds credits Client A disputes. Party B “belongs to a larger group”. A letter from Party B is dated 28 October. |
| Email after the call | The supply contract. Its signature page is hard to read. |

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The packet prepared from it.

**Inquiry packet: Client A, contract dispute with Party B**

- **Status:** Incomplete. Four items missing.
- **Parties for conflict review:** Client A. Party B. Party B’s parent company: missing. The two individuals who signed the supply contract: missing.
- **Missing, and who supplies it:** All four from Client A’s operations director: the billing contact, the parent company’s name, the two signatories’ names, and whether Client A has insurance that may respond.
- **For the responsible attorney:** Party B’s letter of 28 October, as the caller described it. No deadline has been worked out from it. The attorney assesses timing.
- **Routed to:** Conflicts clerk, for the firm’s conflict review, party list marked incomplete. Responsible attorney (litigation), for acceptance once the review is recorded.
- **Next action:** Intake coordinator: review the drafted request for all four items and send it to Client A today.

Prepared for the intake coordinator to review. Nothing has been sent, searched or decided.
