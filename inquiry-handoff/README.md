# Inquiry handoff

Every new inquiry reaches the firm’s conflict review and the responsible attorney as one complete packet on the day it arrives, with each missing item named and someone assigned to supply it.

*Workflow template · Law firms · Operations and process improvement · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflows/inquiry-handoff/)*

## The outcome

A fully powered intake desk turns a web form, a call and a few emails into one packet the conflicts clerk and the responsible attorney act on that afternoon. Every gap carries the name of the person who will fill it. That is 10X what your intake desk can do with the people it has.

This workflow template prepares the packet, lists what is missing and who must supply it, routes it to the people who run the firm’s conflict review and acceptance, and names who acts next. The conflicts clerk runs the conflict review, the responsible attorney decides whether the firm takes the client, and the intake coordinator sends every message. The packet never clears a conflict, accepts a client, gives advice or works out a deadline.

## Who starts the work, and when

The intake coordinator starts it when an inquiry arrives, before anything goes to conflicts, and reruns it as each missing item comes in. The firm administrator owns the checklist and routing list.

## What they supply

- **The inquiry as it arrived.** Form entry, call notes, emails and attachments, unedited.
- **The intake checklist.** The facts required for each matter type, and which related parties go on the conflict list.
- **The routing list.** Who runs conflict review and who decides acceptance, by practice area.

## What comes out

One packet: the matter as described, the parties for conflict review, each missing item with its supplier, what the attorney must see, the routing and the next action. The example is an invented 40-person litigation and employment firm.

Good means the conflicts clerk starts without opening the inbox, and the intake coordinator asks Client A for everything in one message.

## What has to change for your company

1.  **Required facts by matter type.** An employment inquiry needs different facts from a commercial dispute.
2.  **The party rule.** Which related parties go on the conflict list: parents, subsidiaries, signatories, insurers, opposing counsel.
3.  **Routing.** Who reviews and who decides in each practice area, and who covers for them.

Outside law firms, conflict review becomes whatever check your firm runs before it says yes. We map the handoff with the intake coordinator, the conflicts clerk and the responsible attorney, and land each step on the role that owns it. A week of real inquiries proves it before it becomes routine. This workflow template is written for ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot and Gemini, and your firm keeps control of how the work gets done.

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
3. Give your AI assistant the instruction block and the output format from the template, with your own limits in place of the ones the instructions leave open in square brackets. Paste them in as custom instructions in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, or copy this whole folder into a product that loads `SKILL.md` packages. This folder stands alone; you do not need the rest of the repository.
