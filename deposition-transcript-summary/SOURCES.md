Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. From the page-line deposition summary method in CaseMark/skills (Apache-2.0) it takes the idea of sorting a deposition summary by subject, with a page and line reference on every point; a log of objections that records whether the witness then answered; a list of exhibits showing where each came into the record; pointing out where testimony shifts within the deposition or departs from documents and other depositions, with both places cited; a list of gaps to follow up; working only from a transcript that still carries its page and line numbers, and checking for absent pages and reporter's markers before summarising; and answering the lawyer's later questions only with references. Fully Powered AI wrote the version that works from topics the attorney names, replaces every label that judges testimony with a plain note of where two passages differ, treats each errata change as a difference, limits the follow-up list to what the record shows, heads the summary as a draft for a named attorney with a line for each point to confirm, makes a transcript without page and line numbers a stop that ends the run with nothing prepared, and declines to assess, advise or draft. No third-party text is reused.
