# Research plan for counsel: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Quillfeather & Rook LLP, its people, its client Ottermill Tile Co., Fallowmere Ceramics Ltd, Pellwick Auction Rooms, the States of Eastmere and Wendlemoor, the Superior Court of Eastmere, and every statute, case, service and note named below are invented. The research notes deliberately contain an uncertain statute reference, an incomplete case reference and a vague mention of a case, to show how the workflow template handles them. None of them is real, and none may be relied on.

The associate's six inputs come first, in full, then what the workflow template prepares.

## Input 1: the request

Sent by Tomas Reyes-Lund, associate, on Wednesday 11 November 2026.

```

Matter 2026-0412, Ottermill Tile Co. Practice area: Commercial.

my notes from a first look yesterday, and our approved research sources.
Honestly, if you can just give me the leading case on "sold as seen", that
would save me a day.
```

Tomas, a new one for you. Matter 2026-0412, Ottermill Tile Co., commercial.

Ottermill bought a used electric kiln for $18,400 at a Pellwick Auction
Rooms sale on 2 September 2026. The seller was Fallowmere Ceramics Ltd. The
online listing said "fully serviced 2025". The kiln failed on 29 September
and Ottermill's engineer says the heating elements were never replaced.

trial level. I'd like the research done before Friday's call with the client.

Two lines are worth noticing. The listing, the failed kiln and the engineer's report (F3, F4, F8, F10, F11) are tied to no sub-question, because they share no words with the question as Odile Marsh wrote it. The plan does not decide that they matter; it hands them to the lawyer to place. And R1 to R3 appear exactly as the associate wrote them, gaps and question marks included. The plan does not complete the Thrushcombe reference, guess the Marrowgate case or say what either decided; where the notes say what a case held, the plan marks those words as the notes', not its own.
