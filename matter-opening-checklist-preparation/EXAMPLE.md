# Matter-opening checklist preparation: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The checklist for Matter 26-0412.

**Matter-opening checklist: Matter 26-0412, Client A, contract dispute with Party B**

- **Sources read:** Acceptance record. Engagement letter, countersigned. Intake packet, latest version.
- **Matter fields:** Client A. Contract dispute with Party B. Matter 26-0412. Litigation. Responsible attorney as named in the acceptance record. Ready to enter.
- **Billing terms:** Hourly, at the rates in the engagement letter’s schedule. Retainer as the engagement letter states.
- **Documents on file:** Intake packet, the firm’s conflict review record, acceptance record, engagement letter, supply contract.
- **Open item 1:** Billing contact: still missing, requested at intake. Owner: responsible attorney, to raise with Client A’s operations director. Blocks billing setup.
- **Open item 2:** E-billing preference: not recorded. Owner: billing coordinator, once the billing contact is named. Blocks billing setup.

Prepared for the firm administrator, who opens the matter. The billing coordinator sets up billing. Whether work starts before both items close is the firm administrator’s call under the firm’s own rule.
